# Domain Study

## Introduction
Diet and Nutrition Management is a healthcare domain that helps individuals maintain a healthy lifestyle through proper food planning, nutrition tracking, and personalized diet recommendations. A digital platform makes it easier for users to monitor their daily nutrition and achieve their health goals.

## Current Challenges
- Users do not have a personalized diet plan.
- Tracking daily calorie intake manually is difficult.
- Lack of awareness about nutritional values.
- No centralized platform for diet planning and progress tracking.
- Difficulty in maintaining consistency with healthy eating habits.

## Target Users
- Patients
- Fitness Enthusiasts
- Dietitians
- Nutritionists
- General Users

## Existing Solutions
- MyFitnessPal
- HealthifyMe
- Lose It!
- Yazio

## Limitations of Existing Systems
- Many premium features require payment.
- Limited customization for diet plans.
- Complex user interface for beginners.
- Lack of personalized healthcare integration.

## Proposed Solution
The Diet & Nutrition SaaS platform provides personalized meal plans, calorie tracking, nutrition analysis, progress monitoring, and user-friendly dashboards. The system helps users achieve their health goals with simple and efficient diet management.

## Expected Benefits
- Better nutrition awareness.
- Easy calorie and meal tracking.
- Personalized recommendations.
- Improved health monitoring.
- Convenient access from anywhere.
